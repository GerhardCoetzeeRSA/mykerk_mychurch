## Notes on using these

- Always personalize `[naam]` / `[name]` and `[Gemeente Naam]` / `[Congregation Name]` — a template that's obviously copy-pasted gets ignored.
- WhatsApp outperforms email for first contact with SA church admins — lead with WhatsApp where you have a number, fall back to email otherwise.
- Track responses somewhere simple (even a spreadsheet: Church name, contact, channel, date sent, response, status) so follow-ups don't get missed — this matters more than the message wording.
