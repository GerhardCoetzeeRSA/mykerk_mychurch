# MyKerk MyChurch

MyKerk MyChurch is a church platform designed to help congregations manage their activities while strengthening the sense of community and care among members.

Unlike traditional church management systems that focus mainly on administration, MyKerk combines practical church tools with features that encourage connection, prayer, and involvement within the congregation.

## Purpose

MyKerk MyChurch aims to help churches live out the idea of being a caring community — where members support one another, stay connected during the week, and grow together spiritually.

> *A church app should not only manage a congregation — it should help a congregation care for one another.*

## Key Features

### Community & Care
- Prayer Wall for sharing and supporting prayer requests
- Ministries and groups to encourage participation
- Church events calendar to stay connected
- Sermons library for continued spiritual growth

### Church Administration
- Member directory and management
- Church leadership / board information
- Event and ministry coordination
- Administrative settings and permissions

## How It Works

### For Church Administrators
Church administrators register with a 30-day free trial (no credit card required). After the trial, a subscription is required:
- **Monthly:** R500/month
- **Annual:** R5 400/year (R450/month — save R600)

On registration, the administrator receives a unique **congregation code** (e.g. `HOOP-PAARL-347`) to share with their members.

### For Congregation Members
Congregation members join for **free — always**. They use the congregation code provided by their church administrator to register and gain access to the app.

## Vision

MyKerk MyChurch is built to support both the organizational needs of a church and the relational life of a congregation, helping churches become more connected, caring communities.

## Language

The platform supports Afrikaans and English, making it suitable for both Afrikaans and English speaking churches.
